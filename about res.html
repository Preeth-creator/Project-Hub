About The Masala Trail

Welcome to Tasty Bites, where tradition meets flavor! We bring you a flavorful fusion of authentic South Indian non-veg dishes and modern favorites like pasta, grills, and biryanis — all made with fresh, local ingredients and lots of love.

From spicy Chicken 65 to aromatic Hyderabadi Biryani, from rich Meen Curry to creamy Veg Arrabiata — we’ve got something to delight every food lover. Whether you're in the mood for a hearty non-veg meal or delicious vegetarian options, our chefs are ready to serve you a plate of happiness!

Come taste the difference at Tasty Bites — your favorite food stop for every craving.
